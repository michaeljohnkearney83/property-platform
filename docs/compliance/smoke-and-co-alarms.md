# Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms

- **Status:** Draft. Not yet verified against the source by a person or a lettings compliance adviser.
- **Jurisdiction:** England
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-11-20

## Rules

| ID | Rule | When / how often | Blocks? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ALARM-01 | A smoke alarm on every storey with a room used wholly or partly as living accommodation. | Throughout the tenancy | - | [Smoke and Carbon Monoxide Alarm (England) Regulations 2015](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2015/1693/contents), as amended in 2022 |
| ALARM-02 | A carbon monoxide alarm in every room used as living accommodation that has a fixed combustion appliance (excluding gas cookers). | Throughout the tenancy | - | 2015 Regs, as amended |
| ALARM-03 | Check all alarms are working. | On the first day of each new tenancy | New tenancy start (check recorded) | 2015 Regs |
| ALARM-04 | Repair or replace a faulty alarm once told about it. | As soon as reasonably practicable | - | 2015 Regs, as amended |
